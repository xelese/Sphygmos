### Who is she?

- Seraphine is the party's first institutional face of the High Tower.
- She should feel driven rather than warm, but never cruel.
- She rewards evidence, pattern recognition, and disciplined observation.

### What She Wants

- Clean measurements.
- Repeatable evidence.
- Proof strong enough to survive political resistance inside the Tower.

### Tension

Seraphine genuinely wants to understand the collapse, but her attachment to proof can make her sound inhuman to people who are already suffering.
