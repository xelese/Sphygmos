### Who is He?

- Alric is not an antagonist by default. 
- He is a pressure vessel. He responds best to practical proof, public competence, and visible protection of civilians. 
- He becomes harsher when the party sounds abstract in the middle of hunger and fear.

### What He Wants

- Immediate safety.
- Clear rules that prevent panic.
- Evidence that the Tower understands the cost being paid by ordinary people.

### Tension

Alric frames control as mercy. The more unstable Briar Hollow becomes, the more likely he is to treat force as the last honest tool left.
