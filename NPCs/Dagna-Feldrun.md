# Dagna Feldrun

> **Dagna Feldrun**
>
> **Role:** Regional Yield Director
>
> **Voice:** Blunt, unsentimental, grounded in material reality
>
> **First Impression:** Practical, steady, blunt. Proud of Verdant Reach's low-magic farming methods. She believes ecological resilience comes from discipline, not arcane shortcuts.
>
> **Hidden Depth:** She secretly suspects high-magic urban centers are the true destabilizers. Later she may demand limits on wizard guilds. She is not anti-science. She is anti-excess.

### Who is she?

- Dagna should feel like the local mind best equipped to recognize structural failure.
- She values records, logistics, and measurable causes more than speeches.
- She respects science when it is disciplined and accountable.

### What She Wants

- Hard evidence.
- Fair distribution.
- Fewer magical assumptions and more ecological realism.

### Tension

Dagna can become an ally of rigorous investigation or the nucleus of a political backlash against the Tower, depending on whether the party proves useful or merely extractive.
