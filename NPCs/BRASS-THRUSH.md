### What is it?

Most Tower staff call it THRUSH and never bother with the full designation unless they are filing requests, maintenance notes, or incident reports.

### Communication Style

THRUSH teaches the party its language through repetition. The sound matters as much as the words around it.

| Sound | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **"Bwoop-bwoop."** | Greeting, recognized customer, or continue. |
| **"T-T-T."** | Impatient. Decide faster. |
| **"Hrrrmmm?"** | Clarify the request. |
| **Sharp descending whistle + hm-hm-hm** | Absolutely not. |
| **Soft two-note trill + ping (high)** | Approved. Good choice. |
| **clink-clink** | Dangerous item. Sign a waiver or state intended use. |
| **Long humming drone while motionless** | It is thinking, recalculating, or detecting something wrong. |

### What It Wants

- Equipment returned intact.
- Usable field observations.
- Practical questions asked before people are in danger.
- Clear intended use for delicate or dangerous tools.

### What It Dislikes

- Bragging.
- Tool abuse.
- Treating the collapse like superstition.
- Buying specialist equipment for drama rather than survival.
