## Player Reference

### Passive Behavior (While Attuned)

- Near active ley flow, the needle leans toward the strongest direction.
- In stressed regions, tremors can make the needle twitch.

\column

### Active Reads

#### Baseline Sweep
- 1 minute over bare stone or soil.
- Arcana or Survival
  - **Success:** Reveals flow axis and whether it is strengthening or weakening.

#### Pulse Vector Lock
- Triggered when a Pulse or sharp tremor spike hits.
- Arcana
  - **Success:** Gives a clean bearing toward stress redistribution.
  - **Strong Success:** Adds a rough distance band: near, mid, or far.

#### Trail of Thinning
- 1 hour of travel with the compass exposed.
- Nature
  - **Success:** Indicates whether movement is into or out of a weakening gradient.

### Practical Value

- Helps pick safer routes.
- Helps narrow where the next frontier may fail.

## GM Reference

### Passive Behavior (While Attuned)

- Within 1 mile of an active ley-thread, the needle leans along the strongest flow direction.
- In Strained or worse regions, the needle occasionally twitches when tremors occur.

### Active Reads

#### Baseline Sweep (Out of Combat)
- 1 minute, holding the compass still over bare stone or soil.
- Arcana or Survival DC 14
  - **Success:** Learn the dominant lateral flow axis, for example NE-SW, and whether it is strengthening or weakening.
  - **Failure:** The read appears clean, but is mirrored in the wrong direction.

#### Pulse Vector Lock (During a Pulse)
- Reaction when the Pulse hits, or when the tremor spike starts.
- Arcana DC 15
  - **Success:** Needle spins, then snaps toward the stress redistribution zone, accurate within about 10 degrees.
  - **Success by 5+:** Also estimate the distance band to the next failing frontier: near, mid, or far.
  - **Failure:** The compass hunts toward the strongest nearby conductor, such as granite, buried ore, or old pylons, wasting the read.

#### Trail of Thinning (Travel Utility)
- While traveling for 1 hour with the compass exposed.
- Nature DC 13
  - **Success:** Determine whether the party is moving into or out of a weakening gradient.

### Field Use Summary

- Best for route planning around Grey Bloom fringes.
- Strongest value appears when paired with Pulse timing and seismic tools.
