## Player Reference

### Structured Skill Challenge

Players plant 3 rods as a triangle.

#### Per Rod
1. Choose site: Survival or Investigation
2. Secure it: Athletics

#### Environmental Factors
- Hard ground makes anchoring harder.
- Saturated ground increases false-read risk.
- Proper tools improve setup reliability.
- Certain granite outcrops conduct cleaner readings.

### Reading Results

- **3 rods:** Full triangulation and strongest directional insight.
- **2 rods:** Confirms rhythmic interval.
- **1 rod:** Confirms activity, but not full periodicity.

## GM Reference

### Structured Skill Challenge

Players plant 3 rods as a triangle.

#### Per Rod
1. Choose site: Survival DC 13 or Investigation DC 13
2. Secure it: Athletics DC 12

#### Modifiers
- Rocky or frozen ground: +2 DC to secure.
- Soft riverbank or saturated soil: +2 DC to choose site because of false readings.
- Proper tools, such as hammer and auger: +2 to the Athletics roll.
- Near "singing stone" granite outcrops: advantage on the choose-site roll.

### Outcome

#### 3 Rods Succeed
- Full triangulation.
- **Scientific Clue:** Confirms periodicity of about 14-16 days.
- **Scientific Clue:** Confirms directionality, vector inward toward ridge heart or conduit line.
- **Science Points:** +1

#### At Least 2 Rods Succeed
- **Scientific Clue:** Detect rhythmic tremor and establish the 14-16 day interval.
- **Science Points:** +1

#### Only 1 Rod Succeeds
- Detect tremor presence only; periodicity remains unconfirmed.
- **Science Points:** -1
