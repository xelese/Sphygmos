## GM Reference

### Structured Skill Challenge

**Goal:** 4 successes before 2 failures.

Each check represents about 1 minute of calibration.

- Let different PCs own different roles: Tuner, Aligner, Anchor, Brace, or Ground.
- A PC can repeat a skill, but each repeat of the same skill increases its DC by +1.
- Natural 20 counts as 2 successes.

#### Primary Skills
- Arcana DC 15: Tune waveform.
- Investigation DC 14: Align crystal core.
- Smith's Tools DC 13: Anchor base.
- Athletics DC 12: Stabilize during vibration.
- Nature DC 14: Compensate for soil instability.

#### On a Failed Check
- Operator makes DC 12 Constitution save or takes 1d6 force damage.

### Outcome

#### Success
- Gain precise waveform reading.
- **Science Points:** +1

#### Perfect Reading Bonus (4 Successes, 0 Failures)
- **Scientific Clue:** The seismic tremor precedes Vital collapse by about 1.3 seconds.
- **Science Points:** +1
- One-round option at "Moment Before the Pulse": brace the obelisk, abandon it, or grab the core.

#### Failure
- Reading incomplete.
- **Science Points:** -1
