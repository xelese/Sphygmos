# Aqua-Vital Assay Kit

<img src="https://s3.us-east-2.wasabisys.com/campaign-sphygmos-public-images/Items/Aqua-Vital-Assay-Kit.png" alt="Aqua-Vital Assay Kit" style="width:100%; border-radius:8px; margin:8px 0;" />

> - **Purpose:** Test river health and identify whether collapse is happening at the smallest living level in water.
> - **Description:** *A field filter cone with three reagent vials (clarifier, oxygen froth, vital stain), a glass float hydrometer, and a wax-sealed sample tube.*

## Player Reference

### Structured Skill Challenge

Run three tests in sequence.

#### Test A - Contamination Screen (Fast)
- 10 minutes, shoreline sample.
- Investigation or Nature
  - **Success:** No active contamination pattern.
  - **Strong Success:** Trace metals can be detected.

#### Test B - Dissolved Oxygen & Flow Health
- 10 minutes.
- Nature
  - **Success:** Oxygen can read as normal even when life is thinning.
  - **Strong Success:** Estimate fish stress risk.

---
#### Test C - Micro-fauna / Plankton Viability
- Requires two samples: surface + 3-6 feet depth.
- Nature + Medicine (or Nature + Arcana for an Arcanist)
  - **Success:** Assign Aqua Microbe Index (AMI) 0-5 and confirm micro-level collapse in water.
  - **Strong Success:** Infer recent shock vs long decline.

### Aqua Microbe Index (AMI)

| AMI | Condition |
|:---:|:--|
| 5 | Normal micro-web |
| 4 | Thinning + patchy activity |
| 3 | Notable die-off pockets |
| 2 | Deep sample collapse, surface "last gasp" |
| 1 | Rare cysts/spores only |
| 0 | Functional sterility / ecological death |

## GM Reference

### Structured Skill Challenge

**Goal:** Build a defensible water profile and identify micro-level collapse.

Run all three tests in sequence.

#### Test A - Contamination Screen (Fast)
- 10 minutes, shoreline sample.
- Investigation or Nature DC 13
  - **Success:** "No contamination" supports the theory that this is not plague, curse, or toxin.
  - **Success by 5+:** Detects trace metals, which supports metallic taste reports without making them causal.
  - **Failure:** Reagent browns; locals assume poison and social checks worsen.

#### Test B - Dissolved Oxygen & Flow Health
- 10 minutes.
- Nature DC 12
  - **Success:** Oxygen levels are normal even as life thins.
  - **Success by 5+:** Estimate fish stress risk.

#### Test C - Micro-fauna / Plankton Viability
- Requires two samples: surface + 3-6 feet depth (weighted tube).
- Nature + Medicine, or Nature + Arcana for an Arcanist, DC 15
  - **Success:** Assign AMI 0-5 and confirm foundational micro-organism failure in water.
  - **Success by 5+:** Determine recent shock vs long decline through cyst formation or die-off patterns.
  - **Failure:** Sample "goes still" in vial as a Grey Bloom stasis artifact; the reading is unusable, but the phenomenon is obvious.

### Outcome

#### Success
- **Scientific Clue:** The river is clean and the micro-foundation is dying anyway.
- **Science Points:** +1

#### Failure
- **Scientific Clue:** The river water is dirty and maybe poisoned.
- **Science Points:** -1
