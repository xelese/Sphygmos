# High Tower Bastion

{{note
- **Core Premise:** The High Tower levels up as survey teams collect Science Points.
- **System:** Cumulative SP thresholds unlock new bastion levels, wings, facilities, and recruitment tiers.
- **Cadence:** Bastion benefits apply for the duration of each survey. SP is tallied when the team returns.
}}

### What Is the Bastion?

The High Tower operates as the party's bastion. It begins as a Level 4 institution at the start of Survey 0.

:

As survey teams deploy instruments, collect readings, and file successful data, the Tower's understanding of the Vital Gradient deepens. This accumulated knowledge tracked as **Science Points** drives the Tower's expansion. New wings are built. Facilities are upgraded. The institution attracts talent and resources it could not access before.

:

Bastion benefits apply for the **entire duration of a survey**, from briefing to debrief.

### Science Points

Science Points are generated by successful use of scientific equipment in the field. The yield is determined by the equipment's rarity.

| Rarity | Base SP on Success |
|:---:|:---:|
| Common | 0 |
| Uncommon | 1 |
| Rare | 2 |
| Very Rare | 3 |
| Legendary | 4 |

- A **Strong Success** or **Perfect Reading** may grant **+1 bonus SP** on top of the base yield.
- A **Failed Reading** may cost **-1 SP** at the GM's discretion.

:

Science Points accumulate across all surveys and are never spent. They represent the Tower's growing body of knowledge.

### Bastion Levels

| Level | Cumulative SP | SP to Next Level | What Unlocks |
|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---|
| 4 | 0 | 6 | Quartermaster's Wing, Research Wing |
| 5 | 6 | 8 | Expedition Wing |
| 6 | 14 | 10 | Medical Wing |
| 7 | 24 | 12 | Arcane Wing |
| 8 | 36 | 14 | Archive Wing |
| 9 | 50 | — | Apex research capability |

The Tower levels up **between surveys**. When the team returns and files their findings, the Science points tally is updated. If the new total meets or exceeds the next threshold, the Tower advances before the next survey briefing.

:

Advancement benefits do not apply retroactively. If the Tower reaches Level 5 at the end of the current Survey, the party does not receive those benefits. They become available at the start of the next Survey.

### Wings and Facilities

**Wings** are large sections of the Tower campus. They are narrative and organizational — a wing does not have its own mechanic. Each wing contains **facilities** that provide specific benefits.

- **Basic Facilities** are always present once a wing exists.
- **Special Facilities** must be unlocked through narrative milestones or GM discretion.

| Wing | Unlocked At | Basic Facilities | Special Facility |
|:---|:---:|:---|:---|
| Quartermaster's Wing | Level 4 | Storehouse, Workshop | Armory |
| Research Wing | Level 4 | Library, Laboratory | Specimen Vault |
| Expedition Wing | Level 5 | War Room, Stable | Teleportation Circle |
| Medical Wing | Level 6 | Infirmary | Restoration Chamber |
| Arcane Wing | Level 7 | Arcane Workshop | Leyline Tap |
| Archive Wing | Level 8 | Deep Archive | Predictive Model |

### Facility Reference

| Facility | What It Does |
|:---|:---|
| Storehouse | Extra supply slots for survey teams. Gear can be requisitioned before deployment. |
| Workshop | Field repairs and equipment maintenance between surveys. |
| Armory | Improved weapon and armor access for survey teams. |
| Library | Advantage on lore checks (History, Arcana, Religion) during pre-survey research. |
| Laboratory | Process and classify field samples between surveys. |
| Specimen Vault | Preserve perishable or volatile samples indefinitely. |
| War Room | Pre-survey briefing with route intelligence and known hazard maps. |
| Stable | Mount access for overland survey travel. |
| Teleportation Circle | Fast transit to previously secured field locations. |
| Infirmary | Full medical recovery between surveys. Remove 1 Exhaustion per character. |
| Restoration Chamber | Remove curses, diseases, or collapse exposure effects between surveys. |
| Arcane Workshop | Calibrate magical instruments. Minor enchantments for field gear. |
| Leyline Tap | Direct leyline connection. Advantage on first Arcana check per survey. |
| Deep Archive | Cross-reference all survey data. Reveal hidden patterns across regions. |
| Predictive Model | Estimate next Pulse timing with improved accuracy. |

### Surveyor Roster

The Tower maintains a shared roster of trained surveyors. The players collectively manage this roster — they decide who joins, who deploys, and who sits a survey out.

**Roster Pool** is shared across all players. The pool size and entry level are set by the current bastion level.

| Bastion Level | Pool Size | Entry Level | NPC Arrivals |
|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
| 4 | 1 per player | Level 3 | — |
| 5 | Pool + 2 | Level 4 | 1–2 candidates |
| 6 | Pool + 2 | Level 5 | 1–2 candidates |
| 7 | Pool + 3 | Level 6 | 1–2 candidates |
| 8–9 | Pool + 3 | Level 7 | 1–2 candidates |

{{note,text-align:left,background:#aa88aa55
Example: A 4-player party starts with a pool of 4 (one each). At Level 5 the pool grows to 6. At Level 7 it reaches 9.
}}

{{note
**Entry Level** is the level at which a newly created character joins the roster. It reflects the Tower's growing training capacity and the caliber of talent it attracts.

:

**Filling Slots.** When new pool slots open, players collectively fill them by:

- Creating a new character at the current Entry Level.
- Accepting an NPC arrival offered by the GM.
- Promoting an existing hireling or NPC ally the party has recruited through play (e.g., NPCs encountered during a survey who agree to join the Tower).

:

Any player can claim an open slot. The group decides together how to distribute extras.

:

**Deployment.** Before each survey, the party assembles a team from the roster. Each player deploys **1 character**. Remaining roster characters stay at the Tower.

:

**Bench Characters.** Characters left at the Tower between surveys do not stagnate. They train, assist research, or run smaller errands off-screen. Between surveys, benched characters advance to **1 level below** the lowest-level deployed character (minimum: their current level). This prevents the roster from becoming one main and throwaway alts.

:

**NPCs to PCs.** Some NPCs encountered during surveys may become recruitable. If the party earns their trust or alliance, those NPCs can fill open roster slots and become fully playable characters.

:

**Retiring.** A character can be retired at any time to free a roster slot. Retired characters leave the Tower and are no longer available. If the story earns it, a retired character could return as an NPC.

:

**Death.** If a deployed character dies during a survey, that roster slot opens immediately. The player may fill it before the next survey using the current Entry Level.
}}

### Running the Science Point Loop

The bastion loop runs once per survey:

1. **Pre-Survey:** The party uses current bastion facilities to prepare. Issue equipment, apply survey benefits, run briefings.
2. **During Survey:** Track SP as the party uses scientific instruments in the field. Note the result and SP yield for each reading.
3. **Post-Survey Debrief:** Tally cumulative SP. If the new total meets or passes the next threshold, the bastion levels up.
4. **Between Surveys:** New facilities come online. Describe the construction, recruitment, or expansion narratively.

### SP Transparency

Players should know their cumulative SP total. The Tower is a scientific institution — it tracks progress openly. This also lets the party feel the weight of each success and failure.

:

Consider posting the SP total and next level threshold as a visible tracker on Roll20 or as a handout.

### Wing Unlock Narrative

When a new wing unlocks, describe it happening between surveys. These should feel like natural campus expansion, not game rewards.

| Level | Wing | Narrative Hook |
|:---|:---|:---|
| 5 | Expedition Wing | Tower leadership approves dedicated staging. Old lecture halls are cleared for a map room, stabling, and a briefing chamber. |
| 6 | Medical Wing | A physician arrives from a distant city, drawn by the Tower's published findings. An old dormitory is converted into an infirmary. |
| 7 | Arcane Wing | A buried leyline conduit is rediscovered beneath the campus. The Tower retrofits a basement level for arcane calibration work. |
| 8 | Archive Wing | Enough survey data exists to justify a cross-referencing archive. A retired archivist volunteers to organize and index it. |

\column

### Special Facility Unlocks

Special facilities are **not automatic**. They require a narrative trigger in addition to the bastion level. Unlock them when the story earns them.

| Facility | Trigger Example |
|:---|:---|
| Armory | The party recovers a weapons cache or secures a trade route to an armorer. |
| Specimen Vault | The party brings back a volatile sample that cannot be stored in the standard laboratory. |
| Teleportation Circle | An arcanist NPC is recruited who knows the ritual, and the party secures a focus component. |
| Restoration Chamber | A healer or priest is recruited, and the infirmary sees a clear need for magical restoration. |
| Leyline Tap | The party maps a leyline conduit passing beneath the Tower, and a team stabilizes access. |
| Predictive Model | Enough seismic, vital, and leyline data has been collected to build a statistical model of Pulse timing. |

### Running the Surveyor Roster

{{note
**When new roster slots open** (bastion level-up):

1. Announce the new pool size and current Entry Level.
2. Present **1–2 NPC candidates** who arrive at the Tower — each with a name, skill set, personality, and reason for being there.
3. Players collectively decide: adopt NPCs, create new characters, or leave slots empty for now.
4. Empty slots can be filled at any time before a survey briefing.

:

**Scarcity still matters.** Not everyone arrives at once. Some candidates need convincing, arrive mid-survey, or come with conditions. Use NPC arrivals as narrative hooks — a field medic fleeing a collapsing region, a cartographer whose maps contradict the Tower's data, a disgraced arcanist looking for redemption.

:

**Field-recruited NPCs.** When the party builds a strong relationship with an NPC during a survey, that NPC may become recruitable. Present this as a narrative consequence, not a menu. The NPC must have a reason to join the Tower and a reason to trust the party. They fill an open pool slot and can become a fully playable character for any player.

:

**Bench advancement.** Between surveys, update benched character levels: advance each to **1 level below** the lowest-level character that was deployed on the most recent survey. Do not exceed the character's current tier.

:

**Deployment disputes.** If the party disagrees about who to deploy, the Tower does not intervene. The players resolve it. This is their roster.

:

**Roster tracking.** Keep a visible roster on Roll20 or as a shared handout. Each entry should list: character name, player, level, and status (deployed / bench / retired).
}}
