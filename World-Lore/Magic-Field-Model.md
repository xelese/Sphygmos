# The Magic Field Model

> - **Purpose:** Canon reference for how magic relates to the Vital field, how organisms interact with it, and how different classes access spellcasting.
> - **Short Version:** Every living organism drawing Vital Current produces a local field signature. Magic is the learned or innate ability to shape that field interaction rather than merely consume it. Class differences reflect not raw draw strength alone, but how and why the caster couples to the field.
> - **Depends On:** [The Vital Gradient](./Vital-Gradient.md)

## The Electromagnetic Analogy

The Vital system behaves analogously to an electromagnetic system. This is a structural analogy, not a literal physics claim. The relationships map like this:

| Electromagnetic Concept | Vital Equivalent | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Voltage / potential difference | Vital Gradient | The difference in availability between deep reservoir and surface biosphere |
| Current | Vital Current | The usable flow organisms draw to sustain life |
| Conductor / circuit | Organism coupling | The living structure that allows current to flow |
| Impedance | Coupling resistance | How efficiently an organism can draw and sustain current |
| Magnetic field around a current-carrying wire | Coupling signature | The local field distortion produced by a living thing drawing current |
| Electromagnet / field shaping | Magic | Deliberate manipulation of the field geometry around and through the caster |
| Insulation | Internal coherence | The ability to sustain high draw or field manipulation without self-damage |
| Short circuit | Collapse / overcoupling | When draw exceeds what the local field can sustain, coherence breaks |

The key insight is this: in electromagnetism, any wire carrying current produces a magnetic field around it. The wire does not choose to do this. It is a consequence of current flow.

In the Vital model, any organism drawing Vital Current produces a local distortion in the Vital field. This is the organism's **coupling signature**. It is not magic. It is a passive consequence of being alive.

## Coupling Signature vs Magic

These are not the same thing.

### Coupling Signature

Every living organism that draws Vital Current has a coupling signature. It is the local field pattern produced by the act of drawing current through a living structure.

- It is passive.
- It is always present in living things.
- It varies in strength with the organism's complexity, health, and metabolic demand.
- It is detectable by sufficiently sensitive instruments or trained observers.
- It does not require intent, training, or talent.

A tree has a coupling signature. A mouse has one. A sleeping farmer has one. None of them are doing magic.

### Magic

Magic is the deliberate or innate ability to shape the Vital field beyond passive coupling.

Where a coupling signature is the field distortion caused by drawing current, magic is the act of modifying how, where, and in what pattern that field interaction occurs.

The analogy: a wire carrying current produces a magnetic field passively. An electromagnet uses coils, cores, and controlled current to shape that field deliberately into something useful. A spellcaster is the electromagnet.

Magic can:

- align the local field
- focus flow into directed effects
- redirect access across distances
- gate weak or strong regions
- force resonance between field patterns
- distort or scar the field when misused

Magic does not create Vital Current from nothing. It shapes the field through which current is accessible and directs how that current expresses.

## What Determines Magical Potential

If raw current draw were the only factor, the strongest living things would automatically be the strongest casters. That is not how it works.

Magical potential depends on four variables:

| Variable | Meaning | Analogy |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Throughput** | How much current the organism can draw safely | Amperage capacity |
| **Sensitivity** | How well the person can perceive field structure | Instrument precision |
| **Coherence** | How stable their internal coupling is under strain | Insulation quality |
| **Control** | How precisely they can shape alignment, resonance, or gating | Engineering skill |

A barbarian may have enormous throughput but no sensitivity or control. A wizard may have moderate throughput but exceptional sensitivity and control. A sorcerer may have high throughput and innate control but limited conscious sensitivity.

This is why class differences exist. They are not just differences in power. They are differences in how the organism interfaces with the Vital field.

## Class-by-Class Interpretation

Each class represents a distinct mode of Vital field interaction. The categories below use the 2024 Player's Handbook class list.

### Full Casters

These classes have the deepest, most sustained field interaction. They shape the Vital field as a primary activity.

#### Sorcerer

**Mode:** Innate overcoupling.

The sorcerer's body-soul system naturally interfaces with the Vital field at a depth most organisms cannot reach. Their coupling is not learned. It is structural, often inherited or imposed by exposure to extreme field events.

- **Throughput:** High. Their biology sustains heavy draw without training.
- **Sensitivity:** Variable. Some sorcerers feel the field intuitively. Others are overwhelmed by it.
- **Coherence:** Unstable by default. The innate coupling is powerful but not self-regulating.
- **Control:** Instinctive rather than precise. Metamagic represents learned techniques for shaping an inherently volatile interface.

A sorcerer is a wire that naturally carries more current than it should. The field around them is stronger, louder, and harder to ignore. Their challenge is not access but regulation.

Wild Magic sorcerers are the extreme case: their coupling fluctuates unpredictably because their internal coherence cannot stabilize the draw.

#### Wizard

**Mode:** Learned field engineering.

The wizard has ordinary or moderate innate coupling. What makes them exceptional is trained sensitivity, systematic control, and externalized field architecture.

- **Throughput:** Moderate. Wizards do not naturally sustain the heaviest draws.
- **Sensitivity:** Exceptional. Study trains them to perceive field structure others cannot detect.
- **Coherence:** High, maintained through discipline, notation, and ritual structure.
- **Control:** The highest precision of any class. Spellbooks are external field-pattern references, not power batteries.

A wizard is an electrical engineer. They may not carry the most current personally, but they understand field geometry well enough to produce effects that raw power alone cannot.

Spell preparation is the act of pre-aligning internal coupling patterns to match specific field geometries. The spellbook holds the reference patterns. Forgetting a spell means the internal alignment has relaxed.

#### Cleric

**Mode:** Externally ordered coupling.

The cleric's field interaction is mediated through alignment with a divine ordering principle. The deity or divine domain does not inject power into the cleric. It provides a stable alignment framework that allows the cleric's coupling to reach field geometries they could not access alone.

- **Throughput:** High, because the divine framework stabilizes coupling under heavy draw.
- **Sensitivity:** Domain-shaped. Clerics perceive the field through the lens of their alignment.
- **Coherence:** Very high. The external ordering principle acts as a stabilizer.
- **Control:** Broad but channeled. Clerics shape the field along the patterns their alignment permits.

A cleric is a device whose coupling is stabilized by an external reference signal. They can carry heavy current safely because the alignment framework prevents destructive oscillation.

This explains why clerics must maintain alignment with their source. If the external ordering principle is disrupted, their enhanced coupling degrades.

#### Druid

**Mode:** Biosphere-harmonic coupling.

The druid works by harmonizing with the biosphere plate itself rather than imposing abstract patterns onto the field. Where a wizard engineers the field, a druid listens to it and amplifies what is already there.

- **Throughput:** High, scaled to the health of the local biosphere.
- **Sensitivity:** The highest natural sensitivity of any class. Druids perceive biosphere-scale field patterns.
- **Coherence:** Strong in healthy regions, weakened in damaged ones.
- **Control:** Responsive rather than directive. Druids work with the grain of the field.

A druid is a resonant antenna tuned to the biosphere's own frequency. They do not force the field into new shapes. They amplify, redirect, and harmonize with existing patterns.

This explains why druids are deeply affected by Collapse. Their coupling depends on biosphere coherence. In damaged regions, druids feel the loss before anyone else.

Wild Shape is the most radical expression of this: the druid restructures their own organism coupling to match a different biological pattern, temporarily adopting that creature's field signature.

#### Bard

**Mode:** Resonance induction.

The bard uses pattern, rhythm, meaning, and emotional structure to induce stable field resonances. Where a wizard imposes calculated geometry and a druid harmonizes with existing patterns, a bard creates resonance through performance.

- **Throughput:** Moderate to high, scaling with the bard's expressive intensity.
- **Sensitivity:** High, especially for social and emotional field patterns.
- **Coherence:** Maintained through sustained performance. Breaks in concentration break the resonance.
- **Control:** Precise within resonant domains, weaker outside them.

A bard is a tuning fork. They do not carry the most current or understand the deepest field theory. They produce frequencies that cause the surrounding field to vibrate in sympathy.

Bardic Inspiration is a small, targeted resonance pulse: the bard's field interaction momentarily stabilizes or amplifies another creature's coupling.

#### Warlock

**Mode:** Pact-modified coupling.

The warlock's coupling has been fundamentally altered by an agreement with an external entity. The patron does not send power down a pipeline. The patron modifies the warlock's coupling architecture so they can access field geometries that would otherwise be unavailable.

- **Throughput:** Narrow but deep. Fewer simultaneous channels, but each one is potent.
- **Sensitivity:** Patron-shaped. Warlocks perceive the field through the lens of their pact.
- **Coherence:** Externally reinforced by the pact structure.
- **Control:** Limited in breadth, powerful in depth. Invocations represent permanent coupling modifications.

A warlock is a device that has been physically rewired by someone else. The warlock's short-rest recovery reflects this: their modified coupling recharges quickly because the pact architecture is self-restoring, but it only supports a narrow bandwidth at any one time.

Eldritch Blast is the baseline expression of the pact-modified coupling: a simple, reliable field discharge shaped by the patron's architecture.

### Half Casters

These classes combine physical capability with partial field interaction. Their magic is narrower, more applied, and integrated with other modes of action.

#### Paladin

**Mode:** Oath-stabilized coupling.

The paladin's oath acts as an internal alignment framework, similar to a cleric's divine ordering but self-imposed rather than externally received. The oath stabilizes the paladin's coupling enough to produce directed field effects, but the range and flexibility of that coupling is narrow compared to a full caster.

- **Throughput:** High in short bursts. Divine Smite is a controlled current discharge through a weapon strike.
- **Sensitivity:** Low for general field perception, high for alignment-relevant patterns.
- **Coherence:** Very high, maintained by conviction. Breaks in conviction degrade access.
- **Control:** Narrow and focused. Paladin magic is blunt, direct, and powerful within its domain.

A paladin is a capacitor that discharges through a focused channel. They do not sustain complex field manipulation, but they can release stored coupling energy in devastating pulses.

#### Ranger

**Mode:** Environmental field attunement.

The ranger develops practical, place-based field techniques tied to terrain, prey, and motion. Their magic is not abstract field theory. It is lived interaction with local biosphere patterns.

- **Throughput:** Moderate.
- **Sensitivity:** High for environmental and biological patterns, low for abstract field geometry.
- **Coherence:** Stable in familiar terrain, weaker in alien environments.
- **Control:** Practical and intuitive. Ranger spells are field techniques, not engineered effects.

A ranger is a skilled fieldworker who reads the local wiring without understanding the full circuit diagram. Their magic feels less like spellcasting and more like expertise that has crossed the threshold into field interaction.

### Martial and Partial Classes

These classes interact with the Vital field primarily through their own biology rather than through deliberate field shaping.

#### Fighter

**Mode:** Optimized biological draw.

The fighter draws Vital Current with exceptional efficiency for physical performance. They do not shape the field. They use the current their body draws with near-perfect metabolic optimization.

- **Throughput:** Very high for biological draw.
- **Sensitivity:** Minimal.
- **Coherence:** Extremely high. Fighters sustain peak performance under conditions that exhaust others.
- **Control:** None in the field-shaping sense. All control is internal and physical.

Action Surge is the clearest expression: a burst of intensified biological draw that allows the fighter to act beyond normal limits.

**Eldritch Knight** subclass: a fighter who has developed rudimentary field sensitivity and control through deliberate study, allowing limited spellcasting alongside optimized draw.

#### Barbarian

**Mode:** Unregulated overcoupling.

The barbarian draws Vital Current at volumes that would destabilize a less resilient organism. Rage is not an emotional state. It is a metabolic event: the barbarian's coupling opens wide and floods their body with raw current.

- **Throughput:** The highest biological draw of any class.
- **Sensitivity:** None. The barbarian's coupling is powerful but undifferentiated.
- **Coherence:** Paradoxically stable during Rage because the body is fully committed to a single mode.
- **Control:** None. The barbarian does not shape the field. They endure it.

This explains Rage resistances and the inability to cast spells during Rage. The coupling is so wide open and so purely metabolic that there is no bandwidth left for field shaping. The barbarian is drawing so much current that the local field around them is saturated with biological demand.

#### Rogue

**Mode:** Precision biological draw.

The rogue draws Vital Current with unusual precision and efficiency, channeling it into perception, reflexes, and moment-to-moment awareness. Where the fighter optimizes for sustained output, the rogue optimizes for peak moments.

- **Throughput:** Moderate overall, very high in micro-bursts.
- **Sensitivity:** High for immediate environmental awareness, low for field structure.
- **Coherence:** High. Rogues maintain stability through evasion rather than endurance.
- **Control:** None in the magical sense, exceptional in the physical sense.

Sneak Attack is a precision current spike: the rogue channels their full biological draw into a single optimized moment. Evasion and Uncanny Dodge represent reflexive coupling efficiency.

**Arcane Trickster** subclass: a rogue who has developed limited field sensitivity parallel to the Eldritch Knight, applying it through misdirection rather than force.

#### Monk

**Mode:** Internal field regulation.

The monk represents the most sophisticated non-magical interaction with the Vital field. Through discipline, meditation, and physical training, monks learn to regulate their own internal coupling with extraordinary precision.

- **Throughput:** Moderate, but internally redirectable.
- **Sensitivity:** High for internal field perception, moderate for external.
- **Coherence:** Exceptional. Monks maintain internal stability that resists external disruption.
- **Control:** High internally, minimal externally. Monks shape their own coupling, not the surrounding field.

Ki (or Focus in the 2024 rules) is internally regulated Vital Current. Stunning Strike is a disruptive coupling pulse delivered through physical contact. Deflect Missiles and Slow Fall represent real-time internal field adjustment.

At higher levels, monks develop effects that look like magic (Astral Projection, Empty Body) because their internal regulation has become so precise that they can temporarily decouple from the biosphere plate entirely.

## Stress Testing Against The 2024 Rules

The model must hold against known mechanical and narrative edge cases in the 2024 rules. Each case below identifies a potential contradiction and resolves it.

### Anti-Magic Field

**The challenge:** If magic is field shaping, what does an Anti-Magic Field actually do?

**Resolution:** An Anti-Magic Field creates a zone of enforced field rigidity. The Vital field still exists inside the zone, and organisms can still draw current for biological function. But the field cannot be shaped, bent, aligned, or gated. It is locked.

This is why:
- Biological functions continue (creatures do not die inside the field).
- Existing field-shaping effects are suppressed, not destroyed.
- Effects resume when the creature leaves the zone.
- Magic items, which hold pre-shaped field patterns, are suppressed because the rigid zone does not allow those patterns to express.

The analogy: a magnetic shielding cage. Current still flows through wires inside it, but no magnetic field effects can propagate.

### Dispel Magic and Counterspell

**The challenge:** How does one caster's magic cancel another's?

**Resolution:** Dispel Magic is destructive interference. The caster produces a field pattern that opposes and collapses the target effect's alignment. Counterspell is the same principle applied in real-time: the caster reads the forming pattern and produces an opposing resonance before it stabilizes.

The ability check required for higher-level spells reflects the difficulty of producing sufficiently precise destructive interference against a more complex field geometry.

### Wild Magic

**The challenge:** Why do Wild Magic effects happen unpredictably?

**Resolution:** Wild Magic sorcerers have powerful but unstable coupling. Their internal coherence cannot fully regulate the draw. When they cast, there is a probability that the field shaping overshoots, undershoots, or resonates with unintended patterns.

The Wild Magic Surge table represents the range of unintended field geometries that an unstable coupling can accidentally produce. It is not randomness from the field itself. It is the caster's inability to cleanly control what they are doing.

### Cantrips (Unlimited Use)

**The challenge:** If magic costs field interaction, why can cantrips be cast indefinitely?

**Resolution:** Cantrips are minimal field interactions that fall within the caster's passive coupling capacity. They are so small that they do not require significant field restructuring or recovery.

The analogy: powering an LED versus powering a motor. The LED draws so little that the power supply does not notice. Cantrips are field interactions within the noise floor of the caster's normal coupling.

Cantrip scaling with level reflects the caster's growing coupling strength. The same minimal interaction produces a stronger effect because the caster's baseline coupling is more potent.

### Spell Slots and Recovery

**The challenge:** What are spell slots in this model?

**Resolution:** Spell slots represent the caster's capacity for sustained field alignment. Each prepared or available spell requires holding a specific internal coupling pattern. Casting the spell releases that alignment into the field, producing the effect but consuming the internal pattern.

Recovery (long rest for most, short rest for warlocks) is the process of re-establishing those internal alignment patterns.

- **Long rest recovery:** The caster's natural coupling relaxes and resets during extended rest, allowing full re-alignment.
- **Short rest recovery (warlock):** The pact architecture is self-restoring. The warlock's modified coupling snaps back to its baseline quickly, but only supports a narrow set of patterns.
- **Arcane Recovery (wizard):** The wizard uses their spellbook to manually re-align patterns without a full rest, but only partially.

### Concentration

**The challenge:** Why can casters only concentrate on one spell at a time?

**Resolution:** Sustained spells require the caster to maintain active field alignment. This is a real-time control task. Holding one complex alignment is demanding. Holding two simultaneously would require the caster's internal coupling to sustain contradictory or competing geometries, which destabilizes coherence.

Damage breaks concentration because physical trauma disrupts the caster's internal coupling stability. The Constitution save represents whether the caster's biological coherence can absorb the shock without losing the held pattern.

### Ritual Casting

**The challenge:** How do rituals avoid using spell slots?

**Resolution:** Ritual casting is slow, deliberate field alignment performed without drawing on the caster's pre-stored internal patterns. Instead of releasing a prepared alignment, the caster builds the field geometry from scratch using external aids: spoken patterns, gestures, drawn symbols, and time.

The 10-minute casting time is the cost of building field alignment manually rather than releasing a pre-held pattern.

### Resurrection Magic

**The challenge:** How does resurrection work if life draws on Vital Current?

**Resolution:** A dead organism's coupling has collapsed. Resurrection magic re-establishes coupling between the organism's structure and the biosphere field, then uses that restored coupling to restart biological processes.

This explains several 2024-rules constraints:
- **Material costs** (diamonds): gemstones with specific crystalline structures may act as coupling scaffolds that help re-establish the field connection.
- **Time limits:** The longer a body remains dead, the more its biological structures degrade, making re-coupling harder.
- **Willing soul:** The soul must be willing because re-coupling requires the organism's own field signature to participate. An unwilling soul is one whose signature resists re-alignment.
- **Difficulty in Collapsed regions:** If the local biosphere plate is too weak, there may not be enough accessible field for the resurrection to couple to.

### Necromancy

**The challenge:** How do undead function in this model?

**Resolution:** Undead represent forced or distorted coupling.

- **Mindless undead** (zombies, skeletons): The necromancer imposes an external coupling pattern onto dead biological material. The undead does not draw current through its own organism coupling. It is puppeted through the necromancer's field manipulation. This is why it is metabolically wrong and why it disturbs the local field.
- **Intelligent undead** (vampires, liches): These have established a self-sustaining but aberrant coupling that draws current without the normal biological feedback loops. They persist by distorting the field around them, which is why they are often associated with blight, unease, and environmental degradation.

This explains why necromancy fails in Collapsed regions: there is not enough accessible field to sustain even a forced coupling.

### Innate Spellcasting (Racial and Creature)

**The challenge:** Some creatures cast spells without class levels. How?

**Resolution:** Innate spellcasting reflects species-level coupling architecture. Just as some animals have stronger muscles or sharper senses, some species have biological structures that naturally interface with the Vital field in specific ways.

A tiefling's innate spellcasting is not learned. It is a consequence of their biology naturally producing specific field interactions, the same way an electric eel produces a discharge without studying electrical engineering.

Dragons, fey, celestials, and fiends have coupling architectures so far beyond humanoid norms that their natural field interaction produces effects equivalent to high-level spellcasting.

### Magic Items

**The challenge:** How do magic items work without a caster sustaining them?

**Resolution:** Magic items contain stabilized field patterns embedded in their material structure. The crafting process aligns the item's physical structure to hold a specific field geometry permanently or until discharged.

- **Permanent items:** The material structure sustains a stable field pattern indefinitely, like a permanent magnet. The item's physical composition holds the alignment without external input.
- **Charged items:** The item holds a pre-aligned pattern that is released on use and must be recharged, like a capacitor.
- **Attunement:** Some items require the user's coupling signature to activate them. Attunement is the process of aligning the user's personal coupling with the item's embedded pattern. The three-attunement limit reflects how many foreign field patterns a single organism can integrate without internal coherence loss.

### Feywild, Shadowfell, and Other Planes

**The challenge:** Does the Vital field exist on other planes?

**Resolution:** Other planes have their own field systems. The Feywild has an intensely rich, unstable field that produces hyperactive coupling. The Shadowfell has a depleted, muted field that suppresses coupling. The Astral Plane has a diffuse, timeless field that sustains consciousness without biological draw.

Planar travel involves transitioning between different field systems. Spells and abilities may function differently because the local field has different characteristics, not because the caster's ability has changed.

### Barbarian Rage and Spellcasting

**The challenge:** Why can't barbarians cast spells during Rage?

**Resolution:** Already addressed in the Barbarian class section. Rage is total metabolic overcoupling. The barbarian's field bandwidth is entirely consumed by biological draw. There is no remaining capacity for field shaping.

This also explains why Rage grants resistance to physical damage: the body is so flooded with Vital Current that it can absorb and repair damage in near-real-time.

### Monks and Ki / Focus

**The challenge:** Is Ki magical?

**Resolution:** Ki occupies the boundary between biological draw and field shaping. Most Ki abilities are internal coupling regulation, not external field manipulation. But at high levels, monks develop effects that cross into genuine field interaction.

The 2024 rules reflect this: some monk abilities are explicitly magical (Astral Projection, Empty Body), while others are extraordinary biological feats.

In model terms, monks start as internal regulators and gradually develop enough internal precision that their regulation begins to affect the external field.

### High-Level Magic and Collapse Risk

**The challenge:** Does high-level spellcasting accelerate Collapse?

**Resolution:** Yes. This is already established in the [Core Theme](./Core-Theme.md). High-level spellcasting represents intense, concentrated field manipulation. In healthy regions, the biosphere can absorb and rebalance this strain. In already-stressed regions, concentrated magic accelerates the loss of coherence.

This creates a genuine tension for high-level casters in this setting. The most powerful tools available to fight the Collapse are also the tools most likely to worsen it in damaged areas.

9th-level spells are the most extreme case: they represent field manipulations so profound that they temporarily restructure significant portions of the local Vital field. In a healthy biosphere, this is recoverable. In a strained one, it may push the region past a threshold.

## Canon Definitions

> **Coupling Signature:** The passive local field distortion produced by any living organism drawing Vital Current. It is a consequence of being alive, not a form of magic.

> **Magic:** The deliberate or innate ability to shape the Vital field beyond passive coupling, through alignment, resonance, redirection, gating, focus, or distortion.

> **Magical Potential:** Determined by throughput, sensitivity, coherence, and control, not by raw current draw alone.

> **Spell Slot:** An internal coupling alignment pattern held by the caster, released on casting and restored through rest.

> **Concentration:** Real-time active field alignment maintenance, limited to one complex pattern because simultaneous geometries destabilize internal coherence.
