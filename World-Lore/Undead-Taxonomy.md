# Undead Taxonomy

> - **Purpose:** Canon reference for how undead exist in a world governed by the Vital Gradient.
> - **Short Version:** Life requires coupling to the biosphere field. Undead are not the natural result of death. They are interrupted, scarred, or artificially sustained failures of that coupling. Normal death is clean disconnection. Undeath is forced or broken continuity.
> - **Use:** Reference this file when designing undead encounters, Death Knight lore, Hollowed zones, or the theology of the Church of the Last Descent.

## Why Death Is Not Undeath

In the Vital model, living organisms draw Vital Current by coupling locally to the biosphere field. When that coupling fails — whether through injury, age, starvation, or Collapse — the organism dies.

Normal death is disconnection.

The body stops drawing. The nervous pattern ceases. Biological coherence ends. In a healthy region, decay follows immediately.

Undeath does not happen naturally.

It occurs only when the normal failure sequence is interrupted, scarred, or deliberately overridden.

That interruption can happen in three ways:

1. The coupling was damaged rather than severed, leaving a broken feedback loop.
2. Someone or something forces a dead system back into partial function using artificial means.
3. A living person is stripped of coherent draw so completely that they persist in a state that is neither death nor life.

In all three cases, bringing the subject to a stable, field-rich region does not restore them.

The field is present. Their ability to attach to it is not.

It is like carrying a burned-out root system into wet soil. The water is there. The architecture that would use it is gone.

## The Four States

### True Death

The organism loses coupling and dies. In a healthy region, decomposition begins normally. In Verdant Stasis, the body may persist intact because even decay requires surface access.

True Death is the normal outcome. Everything below this heading is an exception.

---

### Hollowed

**What happened:** The coupling was scarred rather than cleanly severed.

Hollowed are victims of traumatic field failure — usually caused by a Collapse event, a magical accident, or prolonged exposure to an unstable zone. Their bodies still move. Their nervous systems still fire. But the draw is broken into disconnected loops, and the person behind the pattern is gone or unreachable.

**What they look like:**

- Repetitive behavior: walking familiar paths, reaching for absent tools, mouthing words with no voice.
- Emotionally blunted. Not aggressive by default, but reactive when patterns are disrupted.
- Eyes open but unfocused. Breathing shallow and mechanical.
- They do not heal. They do not eat with purpose. They do not sleep.

**What they are not:**

- They are not possessed.
- They are not cursed.
- They are not rotting corpses animated by necromantic energy.

**Why stable regions do not help:**

The biosphere field is intact around them. Their coupling architecture is not. The damage is internal — a scarred nervous-field interface that can no longer lock onto the gradient. Proximity to health does not repair the lock.

**How they feel at the table:**

Hollowed should feel like encountering someone mid-stroke who never stopped walking. They are tragic, unsettling, and profoundly wrong without being grotesque. Horror here comes from recognition, not gore.

> *She is still setting the table. The plates are dust. The food is rotten or gone. Her hands move with the careful, automatic rhythm of someone who has done this ten thousand times and will do it ten thousand more, because the part of her that would notice the difference is no longer answering.*

---

### Pinned

**What happened:** Someone deliberately stabilized a dead or dying body using an artificial field anchor.

Pinned are constructed undead. They exist because a practitioner — necromancer, artificer, or relic-engineer — has driven an artificial bridge into the body's residual nervous architecture and connected it to a counterfeit or parasitic source of continuity.

Common anchoring methods:

| Method | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Marrow nails | Iron or black-crystal spikes driven into major bones, carrying a low resonance that mimics coupling |
| Grave-plates | Etched metal plates sutured across the sternum or skull, holding a fixed alignment loop |
| Relic cores | Salvaged fragments of ancient stabilizer technology repurposed as continuity engines |
| Leech-bonds | Parasitic field tethers that draw from a living host or a nearby stable zone |

**What they look like:**

- Stiff, mechanical movement. Less fluid than Hollowed.
- Visible anchoring hardware in most cases.
- No personality. No habits. No residual identity.
- They follow instructions or simple behavioral constraints set during binding.

**What they are not:**

- They are not people. Whatever identity the body once had is overwritten or absent.
- They are not self-sustaining. If the anchor is removed or disrupted, the body drops.

**Why they matter:**

Pinned are proof that artificial Vital Current — or something close enough to counterfeit it — is possible. Every Pinned undead is evidence that someone has solved, partially, the problem the High Tower is still studying.

That overlap is the political danger. The Tower wants to stabilize the field. The people making Pinned have already stabilized individual bodies. The methods are related. The ethics are not.

---

### The Returned

**What happened:** A rare case in which identity, memory, and will are preserved inside an artificial continuity system, but the reservoir is finite.

The Returned are not mindless. They speak. They remember. They grieve. They are who they were, running on borrowed time inside a body that is no longer drawing from the world.

**What they look like:**

- Outwardly almost normal, at first. Skin paler. Movements slightly too deliberate.
- Over time, signs of depletion appear: hollow cheeks, grey-tinged nails, voice losing resonance, reflexes slowing.
- They do not heal naturally. Every wound draws from the reservoir instead.
- They feel temperature but do not regulate it. In cold weather they become dangerously slow.

**What they know:**

- Most Returned understand what they are.
- Some were volunteers — soldiers, scholars, or guardians who accepted the binding to finish critical work.
- Some were bound without consent and woke inside a system they did not choose.

**Why they are tragic:**

Every act of will, every spell cast, every fight survived burns through a reservoir that cannot be refilled from the living field. They are spending themselves. The question is never whether they will run out, but what they choose to do before they do.

**How they feel at the table:**

A Returned should feel like talking to someone who is already mourning themselves. They are lucid, present, and finite. The horror is not what they are, but the countdown they carry.

> *He sets down the pen and flexes his hand. It takes slightly too long to close. He notices you watching and smiles, and the smile is real — tired and real and aware of exactly what the delay means.*

## Why Necromancy Fails in Collapsed Zones

Standard necromancy relies on the ambient field to provide the carrier signal for animation. It shapes the field to puppet a body, the same way combat magic shapes the field to project force.

In a Collapsed zone, there is no carrier signal.

Ordinary necromancy fails the same way ordinary healing fails: the infrastructure it depends on is gone.

Only relic-grade anchoring — hardware descended from the ancient stabilizer civilization — can sustain animation in a fully Collapsed zone. That is why ancient Pinned and Death Knights can operate where modern necromancers cannot.

## Canon Definitions

> **Hollowed:** A victim of scarred coupling whose body persists in broken nervous loops after the coherent self has been lost. Not animated by outside force. Not recoverable by relocation to stable ground.

> **Pinned:** A deliberately constructed undead sustained by artificial field anchors driven into the body's residual architecture. Mindless, obedient, and dependent on its hardware.

> **Returned:** A rare undead whose identity and will are preserved inside a finite artificial reservoir. Conscious, grieving, and running out.

> **True Death:** Clean disconnection from the biosphere field. The normal outcome when coupling fails. In healthy regions, followed by decomposition.
