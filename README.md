# Sphygmos

## Folder Guide

| Folder | What it contains | Use it when you need |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Core-Rules](Core-Rules/) | Player onboarding and table-facing rules notes. | A quick rules refresher, onboarding for new players, or system assumptions. |
| [Items](Items/) | Homebrew item notes plus general D&D shopping references. | New custom item writeups, reusable item catalogs, or level-appropriate shop suggestions. |
| [Items/Homebrew/Scientific-Equipment](Items/Homebrew/Scientific-Equipment/) | In-world survey gear used by the party, often with player and GM procedure text. | Mission gear, structured investigation tools, and field-read mechanics. |
| [Locations](Locations/) | Canonical regional profiles for recurring places, settlements, and points of interest. | Checking region baselines, reusing locations across surveys, or tracking how a place changes over time. |
| [NPCs](NPCs/) | Canonical NPC profiles for recurring characters, portraits, and cross-survey reuse. | Checking who a character is, where they appear, or reusing them in a later survey. |
| [Surveys](Surveys/) | Playable content organized as self-contained survey folders plus shared survey support. | Running the game at the table, navigating a specific survey, or reusing the survey template. |
| [World-Lore](World-Lore/) | Setting truths, campaign themes, and background models of the world. | Tone alignment, canon reference, or deeper setting explanation. |


## Survey Naming Patterns

- `Survey-XX-Name/`: self-contained survey folder.
- `scenes/`: story-facing or location-facing nodes.
- `run/`: GM-facing procedure files, usually in numeric order.
- `handouts/`: player-facing in-world documents.
- Numeric prefixes such as `00-`, `10-`, and `20-` indicate recommended read or run order.
