# HIGH TOWER SURVEY DOSSIER

## Field Contract: Survey "Verdant Reach"

**Recipient:** Any able-bodied volunteer, specialist, sellsword, scholar, priest, exile, or debtor with the sense to travel in a group.

The world isn't ending with fire and prophecy. It's ending with quiet. The Verdant Reach has gone quiet, the wildlife is turning desperate, and the Tower's instruments keep returning... wrong numbers. You don't need to be a hero. You just need to come back with answers (or at least the equipment).

**YOUR ASSIGNMENT**


Travel two days by caravan route to the Verdant Reach (agricultural basin; multi-species settlements; low-magic farming tradition).
Your tasks are simple and measurable:
- Install a Tower-issued Vital Density Obelisk in the region.
- Obtain a stable waveform from the obelisk if conditions allow.
- Collect 
    - sealed samples: soil (3 depths) 
    - river water 
- Interview locals and record timelines of abnormalities.
- Return with the obelisk core (intact if possible) and your notes. 

The Tower's current theory is incomplete. The obelisk is being field-tested to determine whether the Quiet is one underlying phenomenon or several overlapping crises.

You are not expected to solve anything. You are expected to come back with data.

**PAYMENT & CLAIM**
- Hazard pay: **15 gp per surveyor** paid in advance upon signing.
- Return pay: **20 gp per surveyor** upon return with field notes and at least one sealed sample.
- Instrument recovery bonus: **75 gp to the party** for returning the Vital Density Obelisk core intact.
- Usable reading bonus: **10 gp per instrument to the party** for obtaining Tower-usable instrument data.
- Salvage rights to unclaimed goods found beyond settled boundaries (excluding marked Tower property).
- Medical care upon return (limited; triage priority to instrument carriers).

**RULES OF THE ROAD**
- Stay with your group. Lone travel is a eulogy.
- Keep notes dry, instruments secured, and food guarded.
- If a settlement refuses cooperation, you may withdraw-but record the refusal.
- If conflict arises over supplies: de-escalate first. Steel second.

By signing, you accept field risk and acknowledge that the High Tower's duty is to knowledge and survival, in that order.
