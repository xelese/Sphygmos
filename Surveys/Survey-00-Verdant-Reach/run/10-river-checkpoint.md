## Objective

- Collect samples of river water. 
- Interview locals and record timelines of abnormalities.

<a id="political-tension"></a>
## Political Tension

Two groups present here:

- The mob of farmers ~ 30
- The militia guard ~ 10

**Mob**

- The mob is unhappy about the grain seizure and demands more proof that the food they carry is poisoned
- A fisherman reports fish are thinner but not diseased. Since the fish is not poisoned the water cannot be poisoned either

**Militia**

- The militia does not believe the mob. It demands the grain be handed to them so the region does not starve to death.

- The militia is not unreasonable. Alric will listen to scientific results.

<a id="grain-seizure"></a>
## Grain Seizure

**Type:** Social | **Threat:** Medium (mob + militia) | **Delivers:** Not contamination + Alric hook

### Combat

**Type:** Combat | **Threat:** Lethal | **Skill Challenge:** Demoralize or defeat the guards | **Combat shape:** *(CR 4) + (3 x CR 2)* 

- 1 x Druid - Humanoid CR 2
- 2 x Berserker - Humanoid CR 2
- 1 x Guard Captain - Humanoid CR 4

This should be non combat encounter but in case players attack. This will be lethal.

### Challenge

- DC 14 Persuasion neutral -> Convince to allow party through.
    - Each successful scientific discovery lowers DC by 1
- DC 15 Intimidation -> Force compromise.
- DC 13 Insight [Subtle] -> Reveal Alric has lost two children to stillbirth in the past year. He believes something is poisoning the future. His aggression is grief wearing armor. (Advantage on next Persuasion check)

**Success:**
- **Political Alignment:** Alric -1 DC
- He assigns 2 guards for escort later (replacement PCs if PC dies)
- Allows passage to Briar Hollow

**Failure:**
- Grain and food seized.
- Party is refused entry. Go find refuge somewhere else.
- **Political Alignment:** Alric +1 DC

## Water Sample

>*The river is flowing strong but and it looks very clear, unnaturally clear*

Party can sample water via challenge:
[Aqua-Vital-Assay-Kit](../../../Items/Homebrew/Scientific-Equipment/Aqua-Vital-Assay-Kit.md)

### Narration Cues

#### Test A - Contamination Screen

> *The brass cone catches the riverwater in a slow amber sheet. When the clarifier falls in, the sample clouds for a heartbeat, then separates into pale layers that show every bit of silt, sheen, and stray metallic glitter trapped inside.*

#### Test B - Dissolved Oxygen & Flow Health

> *The glass float settles with delicate precision, rocking once before it steadies. A ring of froth gathers where the reagent meets the sample, and for a moment the whole vial seems to hold its breath while you watch whether the water behaves like a living current or something quieter and wronger.*

#### Test C - Micro-fauna / Plankton Viability

> *The surface sample still catches the light with a faint living haze, but the deeper draw is another story. When the vital stain threads through both tubes, one shows tiny drifting sparks and motes that still answer the world around them, while the other hangs unnaturally still, as though life below the river's skin has already begun to forget how to move.*

#### Reading the Pattern

> *None of the individual reactions would damn the river on their own. It is the pattern, laid out piece by piece across glass and reagent, that makes the truth land: the water is not fouled first and dying second. It is dying clean.*

On success, mark on the table:
- AMI 2
- Deep sample collapse, surface "last gasp"
- If the read is especially clean, also mark: recent shock, not long decline
- **Science Points:** +1

On failure, mark on the table:
- AMI unreadable
- Locals assume poison
- **Science Points:** -1

<a id="river-checkpoint-lockbox"></a>
## River Checkpoint Lockbox

**Type:** Treasure | **Threat:** Medium | **Delivers:** Early healing + proof of ration control

>*A militia lockbox sits under a confiscation table, close enough to the emergency grain that anyone opening it is admitting what matters most here: medicine, food, and the right to decide who keeps them.*

- **Method:** Steal
- **Location:** River checkpoint or militia confiscation post
- **Checks:**
    - DC 13 Investigation [Subtle] -> Notice which confiscation crate is a real medical lockbox and when it is left unattended.
    - DC 14 Sleight of Hand or Stealth -> Lift it during the argument without drawing militia attention.
- **Contents:**
    - 1 Potion of Healing
    - [common item roll](../tracker/item-tracker.md)
    - 15 gp in militia script

If the party steals it, increase suspicion around them. Apply **Political Alignment: Alric +1 DC** at minimum, or carry that hostility forward into later instability if the theft becomes public.
