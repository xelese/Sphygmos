## Objective

- Resolve 3 tensions simultaneously, then decide the fate of the region in Tension 4.
- DC increases or decreases based on political alignment + total Science Points.
- Shorthand: AI = Alric, DI = Dagna, CH = Crowd Heat

> **Run This Fast:** Resolve Tensions 1-3 in any order. Let each PC usually commit to one intervention at a time. After those outcomes land, run Tension 4 and then check the outcome table.

<a id="science-points"></a>
## Science Points

- Apply the total as a flat modifier to this scene's DCs:
    - positive total: reduce DCs by that amount
    - negative total: increase DCs by that amount

## Tension 1 - "Who owns the grain?"

**Type:** Social | **Threat:** None | **Skill Challenge:** 1 success

Militia begins confiscations; Dagna refuses to release stores without an audit.

>***Dagna:** We are not dying because we're immoral. We're dying because the world is changing-and 
no one is writing it down.*
>
>***Alric:** I'm not here to win a debate. I'm here to stop a stampede. Besides You hear them? That's not politics. That's hunger sharpening itself. If we don't set rules now, the crowd will set them with rocks*
>
>***Dagna:** If you let force decide who eats, then you've already picked who starves.*

### Support Militia: (Boost Alric / reduce heat)
- Intimidation DC 16: force the hoarders back 
    - AI -1 
    - CH -1 
    - but DI +1 if threats are public.
- Athletics DC 15: physically stabilize a scuffle / separate fighters 
    - AI -1 
    - CH -1.

>***Dagna:** You can't beat scarcity with violence. Violence just chooses a new victim and if you take the grain without accounting, you're not saving the village - you're teaching it theft.*

### Support Mob: (boost Dagna / reduce heat)
- DC 15 Investigation [Subtle] -> Catch false weights / prove the shortage is structural.
    - DI -1 
    - CH -1
- Persuasion DC 15: "audit then distribute" speech to crowd 
    - DI -1
    - CH -1 and if success by 5+ CH -2

>***Alric:** While you count, they burn. While you argue, they take. Truth is expensive. It costs time. We don't have time - look at their eyes.*

### Compromise
- DC 15 Insight [Subtle] -> Read what each side actually fears.

**Success:**

>***Dagna:** Hunger makes liars. And liars make wars. The only thing that survives that is proof.*
>
>***Alric:** I've seen what a 'just a little panic' becomes. It becomes a body in the mud and everyone 
swearing it wasn't their hand*

- Advantage on Persuasion.

Then Persuasion DC 15.

**Success:**

>***Dagna:** Give me an hour and two honest hands-then we distribute with truth, not rumor*
>
>***Alric:** Show me you can keep the weak from getting crushed, and I'll listen to anything you want to 
measure*

- AI -1
- DI -1
- CH -1

## Tension 2 - "The father refuses evacuation"

**Type:** Social | **Threat:** None | **Skill Challenge:** 2 successes

Complication: A father refuses evacuation while panic surges.

>*A father grips the doorway with white knuckles, shielding his family from the flow of evacuees.*

>***Father:** You want me to walk into the Grey with my family and trust strangers with our backs?*
>
>***Father:** My wife can't run. My son can't carry. So what's your plan-leave us behind neatly?*
>
>***Father:** If we're going to die, we die where our names mean something.*

### Skill Challenge
- DC 15 Medicine [Subtle] -> Show the family Verdant Stasis effects are real and healing is "thin."
    - CH -1
    - choose AI -1 (frame as safety/command) or DI -1 (frame as informed consent/data)
- Persuasion DC 15: promise ration share + escort plan
    - CH -2
    - if you publicly promise extra grain: DI -1 but AI +1 (militia resentment)
- Survival DC 15: find safe route / staging point
    - AI -1
    - CH -1

**Success:**

>***Father:** Say it again. Slowly. Tell me who walks beside us.*
>
>***Father:** If I step off this porch... and you lie... I will haunt you.*

- Family evacuates under party's plan.
- Apply effects from successful move.

**Failure:**

- Father refuses movement; nearby families freeze in place.
- CH +1

## Tension 3 - "The records Spark"

**Type:** Investigation | **Threat:** Medium (arson/sabotage) | **Skill Challenge:** 2 successes

Complication: Someone tries to destroy or steal ledgers/samples because Dagna's records decide who gets fed.

>***Agitator (crowd):** See? The militia takes and calls it duty!*
>
>***Agitator (crowd):** See? The ledger-witch counts while your children cough!*
>
>***Agitator (crowd):** They've got charts. You've got empty bowls.*

### Spot it
- One PC makes DC 15 Perception or Investigation [Subtle] -> Identify the saboteur before they act.

### Stop it
If the saboteur is spotted early, any **two successful interventions** stop the sabotage cleanly.

- Sleight of Hand DC 15: swap the ledger case
- Athletics DC 15: tackle/arrest cleanly
- Deception DC 15: misdirect crowd ("that's not the real ledger")

If nobody spots it first, the saboteur acts and the party deals with the failure result immediately.

**Success:**

>***Agitator (if caught):** Order is just theft with a badge. Truth is just theft with ink.*
>
>***Agitator (if caught):** You want the village united? Why? So it can die politely?*

- Sabotage is prevented or contained.
- Apply effects from successful move.
- DI -1, AI -1, CH -1

**Failure:**

- Panic spikes around stores and records.
- Granary door is splashed with oil; a torch hits it.
- Immediate Dex save DC 14 for anyone adjacent or take 2d6 fire.
- CH +2 and both harden: AI +1, DI +1.

## Tension 4 - "The Charter"

**Type:** Social | **Threat:** High (alignment fracture) | **Skill Challenge:** 1 success

This round decides who the village follows.

### Write a Joint Charter (best outcome)
- Persuasion DC 16 with receipts:
    - promise martial corridors + audited distribution + record preservation

**Success:**
- AI -1
- DI -1
- CH -1

**Failure:**
- CH +1
- whichever NPC you publicly contradicted is more likely to flip against you.

### Enforce a decree (choose a side)
- Side with Alric: Intimidation DC 16 or Command Presence
    - AI -2
    - DI +2
- Side with Dagna: Persuasion DC 16 with transparency framing
    - DI -2
    - AI +2

**Success:**

>***Alric:** Rules first, panic second. That's how we keep this village alive.*
>
>***Dagna:** Then write it where everyone can see it. No hidden scales. No hidden hands.*

- Charter or decree is publicly accepted for now.

**Failure:**

- Council fractures in public.
- CH +1

## Outcomes

### Both Help (AI <= -3, DI <= -3, CH <= 4)

>*Alric lowers his blade-barely.*
>
>*Dagna opens the ledger-hands steady.*
>
>*For the first time since the Collapse, the square obeys a plan instead of a rumor.*

Consequence: region stays functional; caravans move with escort; records preserved.

#### Compromise Dialogue

>***Alric:** Give me three rules I can enforce and a place to put my shields.*
>
>***Dagna:** Give me names, measures, and witnesses-so no one can rewrite this afterward.*
>
>***Alric:** Fine. But if someone lights a match-*
>
>***Dagna:** -we record who struck it, and we feed who didn't.*

### Dagna Hostile (DI >= +2)

>*Dagna's voice goes cold. "Then you've chosen force over proof."*
>
>*She steps back from the granary steps like she's stepping away from you.*

Consequence: she begins building the Agricultural Coalition; grain withheld from Tower projects until prioritized.

### Alric Hostile (AI >= +2)

>*Alric's jaw sets. "Then you've chosen paper over survival."*
>
>*His militia closes ranks-not against the crowd, but against you.*

Consequence: martial isolation; Tower writs questioned; you need stealth/bribes to move supplies.

### Both Hostile (CH >= 6 OR (AI >= +2 AND DI >= +2))

>*They stop arguing-because the crowd has become the argument.*
>
>*Alric sees betrayal. Dagna sees tyranny.*
>
>*And the village sees only hunger.*

Consequence: splintered enclaves (matches Verdant Stasis behavior: fracture, violence, refusal to spend resources elsewhere).
