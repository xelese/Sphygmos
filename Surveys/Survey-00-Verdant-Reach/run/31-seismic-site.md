## Singing Stone

**Type:** Investigation | **Threat:** Minor | **Skill Challenge:** 1 success

>*Certain granite outcrops emit a low harmonic hum. It vibrates like distant thunder that never arrives.*

- DC 15 Investigation -> Notice vibration pattern.
- DC 14 Survival -> Determine vibration directionality

**Failure:**

Micro-fracture erupts -> Dex DC 14 or take 2d6 slashing from stone shards.

**Success:**

>*The tremor is not surface instability. It is being transmitted upward through crystalline stone. If you have an instrument you can measure the tremors and gather more info here.*

**Advantage** on locating the placement for Seismic Rods.

<a id="seismic-rod-placement"></a>
## Seismic Rod Placement

Party can determine the tremors via [Seismic-Resonance-Rods](../../../Items/Homebrew/Scientific-Equipment/Seismic-Resonance-Rods.md)

Use the rod item's structured challenge here.

- If **Singing Stone** succeeded, grant **advantage on the first choose-site roll**.
- If the party has proper tools, apply the item's normal setup bonus.
- 2 rods succeeding is enough to confirm periodicity and earn the science result here.
- 3 rods succeeding gives the full triangulation clue.

### Narration Cues

#### Choosing the Sites

> *The first part is all judgment: boots grinding over stone, eyes tracing cracks and runoff lines, hands testing which patches of ground feel solid and which only pretend to be. Even before a rod is planted, the ridge seems to offer better and worse answers depending on where you dare to ask the question.*

#### Securing the Rods

> *Iron bites into the earth with a jarring series of strikes. Each rod goes down with its own note, some clean and ringing, others dull and stubborn, until the three stand at measured points across the ground like tuning forks waiting for a hand to draw music out of the world beneath them.*

#### Reading the Resonance

> *Once the wax caps are set and the ground has had a moment to settle, the array begins to whisper back. Tiny tremors score faint repeating marks into the wax, the crystal sleeves hum almost below hearing, and when the pattern lines up across more than one rod it stops looking like chance and starts looking like rhythm.*

## Ridge Casualty Satchel

**Type:** Treasure | **Threat:** None | **Delivers:** Fallback climbing gear

>*Partway down the caravan path, half-buried under shale and torn survey cloth, lies a satchel someone dropped when the ridge first opened. The buckle is split. The contents are not.*

- **Method:** Find
- **Location:** Granite Ridge, abandoned survey post, or dead courier
- **Locked:** No
- **Trapped:** No
- **Checks:**
	- DC 13 Perception [Subtle] -> Spot torn survey cloth or drag marks between the stones.
	- DC 12 Investigation -> Free the satchel from shale without damaging the contents.
- **Contents:**
	- 1 Climber's Kit
	- 1 Grappling Hook
	- 50 ft. Rope
	- 1 Potion of Healing
	- [common item roll](../tracker/item-tracker.md)
