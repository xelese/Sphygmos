## Objective

- Calibrate the obelisk and secure the core if possible.
- Capture proof that the Reach's symptoms belong to one underlying phenomenon if the waveform stabilizes.

> **Run This Fast:** Run the obelisk item's challenge first. If the party earns the perfect read moment, stop and ask which final choice they make before the Collapse hits.

<a id="hidden-tower-emergency-satchel"></a>
## Hidden Tower Emergency Satchel

**Type:** Treasure | **Threat:** None | **Delivers:** Scientific resupply + foreshadowing

>*Near the chosen site, half-hidden under a collapsed tarp and a scatter of rusted stakes, lies an emergency satchel stamped with an old Tower field sigil. Whoever left it planned for the ground to go bad before anyone else believed it would.*

- **Method:** Find
- **Location:** Near an obelisk site, ruined survey camp, or old field station
- **Checks:**
	- DC 12 Investigation [Subtle] -> Identify the old Tower field sigil and realize the debris pile is deliberate cover.
	- DC 12 Perception -> Notice the tarp edge, stake bundle, or note case before the site is fully unpacked.
- **Contents:**
	- 1 Potion of Healing
	- 1 field note about tremors, ley shifts, or early signs of collapse (Advantage on the first read of the obelisk if found before calibration)

## Calibration

Players calibrate [Vital-Density-Obelisk](../../../Items/Homebrew/Scientific-Equipment/Vital-Density-Obelisk.md).

Use the obelisk item's structured challenge here.

### Perfect Read

Players get one round to act. No DC check. Advantage on the party's first progress check during the escape.

- **Brace the obelisk:** choose data over safety. The party keeps the full waveform result, but whoever braces is exposed when the Collapse starts.
- **Abandon it:** choose survival over proof. The party escapes cleanly, but the frame and site are lost.
- **Grab the core:** choose proof in hand. One PC extracts the core before the frame fails, carrying it into the escape and debrief.

### Narration Cues

#### Initial Calibration

> *The obelisk wakes reluctantly. A tremor passes through the brass frame, the crystal core kindles from dim ember to steady inner light, and a faint shimmer opens around it like the first sketch of a waveform drawn in air.*

#### Mid-Sequence Adjustments

> *With every correction, the machine answers back. The shimmer tightens, bends, or frays; the anchor struts creak against the soil; and the whole apparatus feels one bad motion away from either clarity or collapse. What the crew is doing stops looking like measurement and starts looking like holding an argument with the frontier itself.*

#### Successful Reading

> *Then, at last, the pattern steadies. The waveform hangs clean and unmistakable around the core, each pulse of light following the next with enough precision that the chaos underneath resolves into timing, sequence, and warning.*

#### Failed Reading

> *When the read goes wrong, it goes wrong violently. The frame jerks, the shimmer tears sideways, and force spits from the crystal housing in a sharp flash that leaves the instrument humming with useless, furious energy.*
