<a id="the-quiet-clearing"></a>
## The Quiet Clearing

**Type:** Environment | **Threat:** None | **Skill Challenge:** 1 success per stage

Run both simultaneously.

**Stage A:**

>*When cut open:*
>- *No beetles.*
>- *No larvae.*
>- *No fungal bloom.*

- DC 14 Nature [Subtle] -> Confirm decomposition failure.
- DC 15 Medicine [Subtle] -> Tissue breakdown slowed.

**Success:**

>*Microbial collapse accelerating here faster than farmland. Forest floor normally more resilient - so why is it failing first?*

- **Advantage** on Soil Sample collection Stage A.

**Stage B:**

- DC 16 Arcana [Subtle] -> Roots reacting to energy withdrawal.
- DC 15 Nature [Subtle] -> Root density unusually deep for region.

**Success:**

>*Forest is directly connected to subterranean lattice. The biological network mirrors the geological one.*

- **Advantage** on Soil Sample collection Stage B.

**What you gather is that soil is extremely interesting here.**

<a id="soil-sample"></a>
## Soil Sample

Party can sample soil via challenge:
[Soil-Micro-Lense-Kit](../../../Items/Homebrew/Scientific-Equipment/Soil-Micro-Lense-Kit.md)

### Narration Cues

#### Stage A - Proper Collection

> *The auger turns down through the soil in neat deliberate cuts, lifting out dark layered earth instead of one muddled clump. Each depth smells slightly different once it is exposed, and even before the slides are prepared there is a sense that the ground is keeping a record of what has happened to it.*

#### Stage B - Microbial Read

> *Under the brass lens, the stained sample becomes its own tiny landscape. Threads, specks, and colony-shapes drift into focus one after another, and the viability clock ticks on while you decide whether you are looking at a living web, a damaged one, or the ghost of one that has already gone quiet.*

#### Interpreting the Layers

> *What makes the result unsettling is not the worst sample by itself, but the way the layers disagree. Life still clings near the surface in fragile little signs, while deeper down the pattern falls away into gaps and silence, as though the soil has been hollowed out from below long before the land above noticed.*

On success, mark on the table:
- MDI 2
- Deep layers dead first, surface "last gasp"
- Grey Bloom onset in the forest floor
- **Science Points:** +1

On failure, mark on the table:
- MDI false positive
- Stain cross-reaction suggests toxin contamination
- **Science Points:** -1

## Trapper's Forest Cache

**Type:** Find | **Threat:** None | **Delivers:** Nonmagical control tools + signs of human retreat

>*A slanted roof made of large leaves nearly disappeared into bark and vine. Inside, the remains of a trapper's cache wait exactly where they were hidden, as if the owner meant to return before the woods went wrong.*

- **Method:** Find
- **Location:** Whispering Woods, old blind, or hidden lean-to
- **Checks:**
	- DC 13 Survival [Subtle] -> Recognize old trap lines and the sort of cover a woodsman would use to cover tracks.
	- DC 12 Investigation -> Pull the cache free from brush and bark without springing the trap inside.

**Success:**
- **Contents:**
	- 1 Hunting Trap
	- 1 Net
	- [common item roll](../tracker/item-tracker.md)
	- 12 gp

**Failure:**
- A concealed trap snaps shut while the cache is being pulled free. The triggering creature takes **1d6 piercing damage** and is **restrained** until it or an adjacent creature uses an action to free it. However the cache is free to loot.
