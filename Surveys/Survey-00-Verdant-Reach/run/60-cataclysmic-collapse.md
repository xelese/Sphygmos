> *The world turns hostile. Pressure becomes weight. Stone becomes teeth.*

Escape the collapse before it swallows you.

<a id="objective"></a>
## Objective

- Escape before the Collapse swallows the party.

**Type:** Environment | **Threat:** Lethal | **Skill Challenge:** **Shelter Clock (Earn up to 4 Shelter Tokens)** | **Duration:** Up to 8 rounds (DM discretion)

<a id="quick-setup"></a>
## Quick Setup

Before the first round, do all of the following:

- Show the `Core Rules` handout in Roll20.
- Show the `Shelter Tokens` handout in Roll20.
- Show the `Downed Lost` handout in Roll20.

<a id="gm-summary"></a>
## GM Summary

- The Collapse lasts up to **8 rounds**.
- Each round has a **Global Con Save** and one **Lead Check**.
- A successful Lead Check gives **1 Shelter Token**.
- A failed Lead Check triggers that round's hazard.
- At the end of Round 8, any PC who is **not Lost** survives.

---

<a id="win-lose"></a>
## Win / Lose

### Survive

At the end of **Round 8**, any PC who is **not Lost** reaches the edge of the kill-zone alive.

### Lost

A PC becomes **Lost** if any of the following occur:

- They are still at 0 HP at the end of a round and **no ally dragged them** that round.
- Round 8's Hazard Trigger hits them while they are still in the kill-zone.
- The GM rules they are separated beyond recovery within the round.

---

<a id="round-loop"></a>
## Round Loop

Each round has **two layers**:

### Step 1: Global Con Save

Make a **Constitution save** vs that round's DC.

On a failed Con save:

- Gain **1 Exhaustion**
- Take **2d6 force damage**
- If you **fail by 5+**, gain **2 Exhaustion** instead

> Round 6 modifies this (see Round 6).

### Step 2: Lead Check

Pick **one PC** as the Lead for the round (can rotate freely). The Lead describes how they're getting the group through the collapse and makes **one check** vs the round DC.

- **Success:** gain **+1 Shelter Token** (max **1** per round; party max **4** total)
- **Failure:** no token gained, and the round's **Hazard Trigger** hits (see each round)

### Step 3: Support Actions

Other PCs can do one of the following:

- Help the Lead (advantage if fiction supports it)
- Mitigate the round Hazard (GM sets a check, usually DC = round DC or DC 14-16)
- Spend resources (spells/items/features) to negate hazards, prevent separation, or protect a downed ally

---

<a id="shelter-tokens"></a>
## Shelter Tokens

**Spend 1 Shelter Token (any time during the round) to do ONE:**

- **Auto-pass** one PC's **Global Con save** for this round, **or**
- On a failed Global Con save: prevent the **Exhaustion gain** (they still take **2d6 force**), **or**
- **Negate** one instance of the round's **Hazard Trigger** affecting one PC

---

<a id="downed-and-lost"></a>
## Downed And Lost

If a PC drops to **0 HP** during the Collapse:

- They are **Downed** as normal.
- If **no ally uses their action** that round to **Drag** them, they become **Lost** at end of the round.

**Drag (action):**

- You move the downed PC with you up to your speed (or half speed if you prefer).
- While dragging, the Lead check is made at **disadvantage**.

<a id="round-details"></a>
## Round Details

<a id="round-1-pressure-shift-dc-10"></a>
## Round 1 - Pressure Shift (DC 10)
**Scene:** The obelisk spikes violently; debris hammers the ground.

**Hazard Trigger:**
- Shrapnel / dust blast: one exposed PC takes a minor hit (GM chooses: small damage or disadvantage on next roll).

---

<a id="round-2-ground-fracture-dc-11"></a>
## Round 2 - Ground Fracture (DC 11)
**Scene:** Stone cracks; wells collapse; footing turns to sliding plates.

**Hazard Trigger:**
- **Dex save DC 14** or fall **prone** (GM can apply to the Lead, the rear-guard, or anyone on bad footing).
- If the Lead is prone, the next Escape Progress check is made with **disadvantage** unless someone spends their action to stabilize them.

---

<a id="round-3-neural-stress-dc-12"></a>
## Round 3 - Neural Stress (DC 12)
**Scene:** Your nerves fire wrong. Structures feel like predators.

**Hazard Trigger:**
- **Wisdom save DC 14**
  **Failure:** **Frightened of the nearest large structure** until end of next round (instinctive panic slows you down).
  - While frightened this way, you cannot be the Lead unless another PC is physically guiding you.

---

<a id="round-4-metabolic-collapse-dc-13"></a>
## Round 4 - Metabolic Collapse (DC 13)
**Scene:** Blood thickens. Breath becomes labor. Magic feels thin.

**Rule Change (always active this round):**
- **Healing magic restores only 25%** of its usual amount.

**Hazard Trigger:**
- Any creature **above half HP** makes an additional **Con save DC 14**
  **Failure:** fall **prone**, vomit blood, and take **1d6 force damage**.

---

<a id="round-5-vital-floor-breach-dc-14"></a>
## Round 5 - Vital Floor Breach (DC 14)
**Scene:** The ground "takes" energy-like your body is paying a toll.

**Hazard Trigger:**
- Each PC automatically loses **1 Hit Die worth of HP** (no save).
  - Roll one of your Hit Dice and add Con mod; subtract that much HP.
  - This damage can drop a PC to 0 HP.

---

<a id="round-6-grey-bloom-wave-dc-15"></a>
## Round 6 - Verdant Stasis Wave (DC 15)
**Scene:** A wave rolls through the area-air goes wrong, sound warps.

**Rule Change (start of round):**
- **All concentration ends.**
- Failed Global Con saves this round give **2 Exhaustion** instead of 1.

**Hazard Trigger:**
- The wave "kicks" one PC sideways: separated / pinned / slowed (GM picks a fitting complication).
  A successful mitigation check by an ally can cancel it.

---

<a id="round-7-aftershock-implosion-dc-16"></a>
## Round 7 - Aftershock Implosion (DC 16)
**Scene:** A structure collapses inward like it's folding itself.

**Hazard Trigger:**
- **Dex save DC 16** or take **4d6 bludgeoning damage** from collapse.

---

<a id="round-8-total-collapse-dc-17"></a>
## Round 8 - Total Collapse (DC 17)
**Scene:** The zone fails completely.

**Hazard Trigger:**
- **Immediate drop to 0 HP.**
