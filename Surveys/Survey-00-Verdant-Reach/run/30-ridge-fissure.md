## Ridge Fissure

**Type:** Environment | **Threat:** Lethal | **Skill Challenge:** 3 successes

> *A newly formed crack splits the party's path. It's 15 feet wide in places. Depth unknown. Wind rises from below — warm and wrong, carrying the faint mineral smell of deep stone that should never reach the surface.*

>*What do you do?*

**Initial Save:** Dex save 10 or suffer **disadvantage** on skill checks.

- Nature DC 14 -> Predict tremor timing.
- Investigation DC 15 -> Identify stable rock.
- Athletics DC 15 -> Secure rope anchors.
- Acrobatics DC 14 -> Cross unstable ledge.

**On 3 failures:**
Major fracture -> Everyone takes 4d6 damage and falls (climbing required).
