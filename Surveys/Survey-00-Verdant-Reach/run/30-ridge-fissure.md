# Ridge Fissure

## Objective

- Survive the ridge fissure.

## Ridge Fissure

**Type:** Environment | **Threat:** Lethal | **Skill Challenge:** 3 successes

> *A newly formed crack splits the party's path. It's 15 feet wide in places. Depth unknown. Wind rises from below - warm. You see molten rock*

>*What do you do?*

**Initial Save:** Dex save 10 or suffer **disadvantage** on skill checks.

- Athletics DC 15 -> Secure rope anchors.
- Acrobatics DC 14 -> Cross unstable ledge.
- Investigation DC 15 -> Identify stable rock.
- Nature DC 14 -> Predict tremor timing.

**On 3 failures:**
Major fracture -> Random PC Dex DC 15 or fall (4d6 damage, climbing required).

## End
