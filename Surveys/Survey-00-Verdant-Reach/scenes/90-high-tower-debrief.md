## Introduction

> *The journey back takes 2 days.*
> *By the time the High Tower rises over the horizon, it feels impossibly tall. The High Tower does not celebrate you.*
> 
> *It catalogs you.*
> 
> *You are led into the Convergence Chamber within the research wing - ringed with lenses, brass rings, and suspended crystal plates. Scribes stand ready with ink already wet on their quills, as if your lives were a conclusion they expected.*
> *At the center, a table of dark stone waits. On it: your instruments, your notes, your blood-stained field sheets, and whatever you managed to drag home intact.*
> *A senior archivist speaks without warmth, not because they are cruel-because warmth is a resource and the Tower is rationing everything.*
> 
> *"Survey ledger... open."*

Give the players a moment.

Then:

>*"The Verdant Reach is now classified Grey Bloom."*

The words feel bureaucratic. Wrong and small.

## Findings Ledger

Start with 0 and add 1 point per success. Each point allows them to claim one benefit in the next survey.

## Soil Micro-Lens Kit
*Microbial collapse*

**Success: Clean Depth Profile**
> Soil samples: three depths. Proper controls. Repeatable.
>
> The archivist's finger traces your chart like a prayer they don't believe in.
>
> "The foundation is failing from below. Not poison. Not blight. Not curse."
>
> A scholar nearby whispers-too softly to be brave: "The world is starving... from the bottom."

**Failure: Compromised / Lost**
> Soil samples: compromised. Cross-reactive stain. Unusable.
>
> The archivist pauses, then speaks the most Tower sentence imaginable: "This does not mean the phenomenon is false."
>
> A long beat. "It means we cannot convince anyone."

## Aqua-Vital Assay
*River "poison" paranoia*

**Success: Clean Water, Dying Micro-Fauna**
> Water is clean.
>
> "And yet the living foundation in it is thinning."
>
> The archivist's voice hardens for the first time. "You did not find an enemy upstream. You found absence."

**Failure: Reagent Browns / Locals 'Confirmed Poison'**
> Water tests: compromised. Field reaction produced a false contamination signature.
>
> The archivist doesn't scold you. That would be human.
>
> "This error cost us weeks."
>
> A second voice adds, coldly: "And it gave the factions a story they wanted."

## Seismic Resonance Rods
*Periodicity / "it's rhythmic"*

**Success: Full Triangulation**
> Seismic rods: three nodes. Clear triangulation.
>
> A projection blooms overhead-three points, a triangle, and a vector that seems to point inward as if the world is inhaling.
>
> "Interval confirmed."
>
> A scholar swallows. "It isn't random."
>
> The archivist finishes the thought: "It's a schedule."

**Failure: One Rod / Cracked / No Periodicity**
> Seismic rods: insufficient. Pattern contested.
>
> Someone in the back laughs once-then stops, ashamed.
>
> The archivist's eyes never leave the page. "The ground still trembles. The world still thins. And now we have less proof to spend."

## Vital Density Obelisk
*Waveform / prediction / that terrifying precision*

**Success: Precise Waveform**
> The obelisk core is set into a brass cradle.
>
> For a breath it does nothing.
>
> Then the crystal catches light and a waveform crawls into existence-tightening, tightening, tightening-like a fist closing.
>
> "You obtained a clean contraction profile," the archivist says. "We can predict the next event."
>
> They look up. "Not prevent. Predict."

**Bonus Success: Perfect Reading (No Failures)**
> Addendum: phase relation confirmed.
>
> The archivist's voice drops, almost unwilling. "Seismic lead time... one point three seconds."
>
> No one speaks.
>
> Because one point three seconds isn't warning. It's a guillotine timer.

**Failure: Cracked / Useless Core**
> Obelisk core: fractured. Catastrophic drift.
>
> The archivist closes the ledger for a moment. "You witnessed the truth."
>
> Their gaze is flat as stone. "But you did not bring it home."

## Verdant Reach - Political Stability

### Captain Alric Venn (Militia / Order)

**ALLY** (they mediated, earned his respect):
> "Captain Venn complied," the archivist reads.
> "He kept order when the world stopped behaving."
> In the margins, someone has written: *He believed them-eventually.*

**NEUTRAL** (uneasy truce):
> "Captain Venn tolerated the Tower."
> "He rationed hard. He escorted caravans. He seized grain."
> The archivist pauses.
> "History will call him a villain or a savior depending on which side ate."

**ENEMY** (they failed him / abandoned the crisis):
> "Captain Venn declared isolation."
> "Tower property was detained."
> "Field teams were turned away."
> The archivist doesn't add drama.
> The drama is the sentence itself:
> "And the region learned to survive without you."

### Dagna Feldrun (Agronomy / Reform)

**ALLY** (they backed her reforms):
> Dagna doesn't cry. She never did.
> "We stop feeding the machine," she says. "Or the machine eats the world."
> The archivist's note is simple:
> "Agrarian compliance increased. Arcane dependence decreased."

**NEUTRAL** (they didn't champion her):
> "Yield Director Feldrun remained unconvinced."
> "She did not sabotage."
> "She did not assist beyond minimum."
> The archivist's last line is almost sympathetic:
> "She chose her people first. As most do."

**ENEMY** (they made her a foe):
> "Feldrun formed the Agricultural Coalition."
> "Grain withheld from Tower projects."

## Seraphine's Closing Monologue

Run this after reviewing the data. Use the lines below as modular pieces depending on what the party actually proved. Seraphine should only speak with certainty about what the players successfully established; on failed experiments, she should note uncertainty, contestation, or suspicion rather than confirmed fact.

### Opening
> *Later that night, in a smaller chamber.*
>
> *Charts pinned to walls. Crystal fragments scattered across tables. Seismic maps overlaid with leyline geometry.*
>
> *Seraphine stands before the projection. She looks exhausted. Not frightened. Focused.*

### If Soil Micro-Lens Kit succeeded
> *"The substrate is collapsing first."*
>
> *"Not from rot. Not from taint. From depletion at the smallest living tier."*

### If Soil Micro-Lens Kit failed
> *"The soil evidence is incomplete."*
>
> *"I suspect the foundation is failing from below, but suspicion is not proof."*

### If Aqua-Vital Assay succeeded
> *She gestures to a river cross-section marked in fading blue.*
>
> *"The water is clean."*
>
> *"What is dying in it is smaller than panic knows how to name."*
>
> *"There is no enemy upstream. There is absence."*

### If Aqua-Vital Assay failed
> *She sets the water notes aside with visible irritation.*
>
> *"The contamination claim may be false, but I cannot yet disprove it cleanly."*
>
> *"Right now, fear has better evidence than we do."*

### If Seismic Resonance Rods succeeded
> *She overlays three points beneath the Reach. A triangle blooms. Then an inward vector.*
>
> *"It isn't random."*
>
> *"The interval is real."*
>
> *"This was not an isolated event. It was part of a pattern."*

### If Seismic Resonance Rods failed
> *She adjusts the seismic slate, then stops before finalizing the projection.*
>
> *"The tremors may be rhythmic."*
>
> *"May be. That word will matter to everyone who wants permission to wait."*

### If Vital Density Obelisk bonus success triggered
> *She taps the phase mark with one fingernail.*
>
> *"Seismic lead time: one point three seconds."*
>
> *"That is not warning. It is a countdown."*

### If Vital Density Obelisk succeeded
> *Her hand rests on the stabilized obelisk core.*
>
> *"The waveform contracts before release."*
>
> *"We can predict the next event."*
>
> *She looks up then, with something harsher than fear.*
>
> *"Not stop it. Predict it."*

### If Vital Density Obelisk failed
> *The cracked core sits on the table between you like a tooth pulled from a god.*
>
> *"I saw enough to fear it,"* she says quietly.
>
> *"Not enough to model it."*

### If Soil + Water both succeeded
> *"The collapse is biological before it is visible."*
>
> *"The world is starving from the bottom up."*

### If Seismic + Obelisk both succeeded
> *She overlays projected cycles. Each one larger. Each interval shorter.*
>
> *"If the pattern holds, the next Pulse will exceed this one."*
>
> *"And the one after that will exceed the next."*

### Final close

> *She lets the layered projections overlap-soil, water, tremor, light.*
>
> *"Disease does not explain this. Curse does not explain this. Contamination does not explain this."*
>
> *"This is systemic."*
>
> *"Something below the Reach is discharging through the whole living structure."*
>
> *She looks to the window instead of the charts.*
>
> *"We did not witness a disaster."*
>
> *"We witnessed a process."*
>
> *A beat.*
>
> *"Maybe even a heartbeat."*

|:---|:---|:---|
| Soil Micro-Lens Kit (Uncommon) | Success / Failure | +1 / 0 |
| Aqua-Vital Assay Kit (Uncommon) | Success / Failure | +1 / -1 |
| Seismic Resonance Rods (Uncommon) | Success / Failure | +1 / 0 |
| Vital Density Obelisk (Rare) | Success / Failure | +2 / 0 |
| Vital Density Obelisk | Perfect Reading Bonus | +1 |
| | **Maximum possible** | **6** |

If cumulative SP reaches **6**, the Tower advances to **Level 5** before Survey 1. Level 5 benefits (Expedition Wing, +1 Camp Point for Tower gear, pre-survey briefings, and 2 new roster slots) become available at the start of Survey 1.

## Roster Update

If the party earned Alric Venn's trust (ALLY status), he offers **2 militia members** willing to transfer to Tower service. These NPCs can fill open roster slots at Level 5 and become fully playable characters.

If Alric is NEUTRAL or ENEMY, this offer does not happen.
