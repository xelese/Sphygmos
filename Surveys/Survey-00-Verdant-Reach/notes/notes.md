# Survey-00 Notes

## GM Notes

### Use Of This Section

This file tracks what the Tower actually learns from Survey 00.

- Do not promote world-lore truth into play unless the party earned the evidence.
- Seraphine may suspect more than she can prove.
- It is valid for Survey 00 to end with no major theory confirmed.

### Science Outcome Reference

Maximum possible Survey 00 science yield is **6 SP**:

- Soil Micro-Lens Kit: **+1 SP**
- Aqua-Vital Assay Kit: **+1 SP**
- Seismic Resonance Rods: **+1 SP**
- Vital Density Obelisk: **+2 SP**
- Vital Density Obelisk perfect reading bonus: **+1 SP**

The High Tower begins Survey 00 at **Level 4**.

- **0-5 total SP after debrief:** Tower remains **Level 4**.
- **6 total SP after debrief:** Tower advances to **Level 5** before Survey 1.

### Knowledge States After Survey 00

#### If The Party Brings Back No Credible Science

Use this if the party loses, ruins, or fatally compromises every major reading.

- The Tower confirms only that the Verdant Reach crisis is real and worsening.
- Seraphine remains unconvinced that she can defend any unified model in front of the Tower.
- The obelisk does not prove a measurable field.
- The phrase **Vital Gradient** should not return to active Tower use.

#### If The Party Brings Back Partial Evidence

Use this if one or more instruments succeed, but the obelisk does not produce a clean read.

- The Tower can argue that the crisis is systemic rather than a simple plague, curse, or poisoning.
- Soil, water, or seismic evidence may each narrow the field of explanation.
- Seraphine suspects a hidden structure behind the collapse, but cannot prove a field cleanly.
- She should speak in terms of patterns, depletion, timing, or relation rather than plates and gradient as established theory.

#### If The Obelisk Succeeds

Use this if the Vital Density Obelisk produces a clean reading.

- The Tower proves there is a measurable field tied to the Reach's decline.
- The Tower can defend the claim that the tremors, ecological thinning, and Collapse are one connected phenomenon.
- Seraphine now has enough evidence to argue that the field is structured rather than diffuse.
- She can begin advancing the lower-plate / upper-plate / gradient model inside the Tower.
- She still cannot prove why the field exists, what drives it, or how spellwork truly relates to it.

#### If The Obelisk Perfect Reading Bonus Also Triggers

Use this only on a full clean obelisk success.

- The Tower learns that the field contracts before Collapse.
- The seismic lead time is about **1.3 seconds**.
- Prediction becomes barely possible, but prevention remains far out of reach.

### What Survey 00 Never Proves By Itself

Even on a perfect survey, Survey 00 does **not** prove any of the following:

- why the field exists
- what sustains the lower plate
- whether life directly draws from the field
- exactly how magic relates to the field
- whether the Survey 00 model applies cleanly to every region
